BooleanIQ Uploader

A private upload and scheduling tool for the BooleanIQ YouTube channel.

Uses YouTube API Services. This tool uploads videos to YouTube through the YouTube Data API. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy and Terms.

What it does

BooleanIQ makes short, sourced videos in Hinglish that warn Indian viewers about online scams and explain AI and technology in plain language. BooleanIQ Uploader is the small tool the channel owner uses to publish those finished videos on a regular schedule instead of uploading each one by hand in YouTube Studio.

  • Uploads a finished video to the channel owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Sets the title, description, tags and category that the owner wrote.
  • Sets the visibility the owner chose (public, private or unlisted) and, for scheduled videos, the time YouTube should make it public.
  • Declares altered or synthetic content for videos that use AI-generated footage or an AI-cloned voice, and marks videos as not made for kids.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It runs on the owner's own computer, signs in with the owner's own Google account through Google's standard OAuth consent screen, and is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else.

What it does not do

It does not read or store data about viewers, comments, other channels or analytics, and it does not share any data with third parties.
