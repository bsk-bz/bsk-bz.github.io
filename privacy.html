BooleanIQ Uploader

Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. Last updated 16 November 2026.

What this app is

BooleanIQ Uploader is a private, single-user tool run by the owner of the BooleanIQ YouTube channel. It uploads videos that the channel owner has produced to the channel owner's own YouTube channel, with the title, description, tags, privacy setting and publish time the owner has chosen. It is not offered to the public and no other user can sign in to it.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the signed-in user's own YouTube channel (scope youtube.upload).
  • Read-only access to the signed-in user's own channel name and the status of videos it uploaded (scope youtube.readonly), used only to confirm which channel is signed in and that an upload succeeded.

The app does not read, collect or store data about any other YouTube user, viewer, comment or channel.

How data is used and stored

  • The OAuth access token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a file readable only by that user. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • The app keeps a local log of its own uploads (video ID, title and scheduled time) on the owner's computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or combined with data from other sources.

Uploads and visibility

Before each upload the owner chooses whether the video is public, private or unlisted, and may schedule it to become public at a set time. Videos that include AI-generated footage or an AI-cloned voice are declared as altered or synthetic content.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer removes all stored authorization data. To request deletion of any data held by this app, contact the address below; it will be deleted within 7 days.
